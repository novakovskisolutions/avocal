Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and we never ask for your email. Your food log, weight history and settings are stored on your phone, so if you uninstall the app or lose the phone, that data goes with it.

How accurate are the calorie estimates?

They are AI estimates from a photo or a description, so treat them as a good guide, not a lab result. Mixed dishes and portion sizes are the hardest part. Adding a few words about the portion helps.

What happens to my photos?

Your photo is sent securely to Google's Gemini AI to work out the estimate. We do not keep it. The Privacy Policy explains how Google may handle it, so please read it before you start.

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Google Play, go to Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose Avo Cal and tap Cancel subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

Is Avo Cal medical advice?

No. Avo Cal gives general nutrition information and estimates. It does not replace a doctor or dietitian, and it is not suitable for managing medical conditions, allergies or eating disorders.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 or newer.

Where is Avo Cal available?

Avo Cal is currently not offered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland.
