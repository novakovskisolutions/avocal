Novakovski Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Avo Cal mobile app and this website, https://novakovskisolutions.github.io/avocal/ (together, the "Service"). This policy explains what information the Service handles, why, and the choices you have. Our Terms of Service also apply. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

Avo Cal does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and the app has no social login.

Information we handle

Meals you log and your body details (stored on your phone). Your food log, calories and macros, weight entries, age, height, sex, activity level, goals, streaks, reminders and saved meal plan are stored on your device. We do not receive a copy of them, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Photos and meal descriptions (sent for AI analysis). When you take or upload a meal photo, or describe a meal in text, it is sent to our server and on to Google Gemini to estimate calories and nutrition. We do not keep these photos or descriptions.

Chat and meal plans (sent for AI answers). When you chat with Avo or generate a meal plan, the app sends your message together with the context needed for a useful answer: your age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal, daily calorie target, what you have logged today, and your saved plan if you have one. This is sent through our server to Google Gemini. We do not keep your chat messages; the conversation is held in the app's memory and is cleared when the app is closed or you start a new chat.

Anonymous ID and technical data. To protect the Service from abuse and apply daily usage limits, the app signs in anonymously using Firebase (Google). This creates a random ID that is not linked to your name or email. We also use Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity to confirm that requests come from the genuine app, which involves device and app integrity signals. Our server keeps a count of AI requests per anonymous ID per day, and technical logs (anonymous ID, time, AI model used, result status). Logs do not contain your photos, meal text or chat messages.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status. We never see your payment card or Google account payment details.

Your own API key (optional). If you choose to add your own Gemini API key in Settings, it is stored only on your phone, and AI requests then go directly from your phone to Google instead of through our server.

This website. The website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it loads no third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Health-related information and disclaimer

Calorie, weight and food information can reveal health-related details. We use it only to provide the features you ask for, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Avo Cal and its chat mascot Avo provide general nutrition information and estimates. Photo-based calorie estimates can be wrong. The Service is not medical advice and is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have or may be at risk of an eating disorder, please talk to a qualified professional before changing how you eat.

How we use information

  • To provide and operate the Service, including AI meal estimates, chat and meal plans
  • To apply usage limits, keep the Service secure, and find and prevent fraud and abuse
  • To process and verify subscriptions and give you access to premium features
  • To fix problems, and to improve and develop the Service
  • To answer your messages and requests
  • To comply with legal obligations

Google Gemini and how Google uses AI data

We use the Google Gemini API to analyze meal photos, estimate nutrition, answer chat questions and create meal plans. Google acts as a provider of this processing for us.

Depending on the Gemini API plan we use at the time, Google may use the content sent to Gemini (including photos and messages) to improve its products and services, and that content may be reviewed by humans. On Google's paid plans, Google states that it does not use this content to improve its products. Please do not include faces, names, documents or other sensitive information in photos or messages. You can ask which plan we currently use by emailing novakovskisolutions@gmail.com.

Sale of personal information

Novakovski Solutions does not sell, rent, lease or trade your personal information, meal photos, usage data or purchase information to third parties or data brokers. The app does not show advertising and does not include advertising trackers.

Third-party services

  • Google Gemini API, Google Cloud and Firebase: AI processing, anonymous sign-in, app verification, server hosting and usage counters
  • Google Play (Billing and Play Integrity): purchases and app verification
  • RevenueCat: subscription management
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of this website

Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

International transfers

Our providers operate globally, so information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on the safeguards offered by these providers, such as standard contractual clauses.

Data retention

  • Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Photos, meal descriptions and chat messages are not kept by us. Google retains them according to its own terms.
  • Per-day usage counters tied to your anonymous ID are deleted automatically after a few days.
  • Technical logs are kept for a limited period (by default about 30 days).
  • Purchase and subscription records are kept as long as needed to manage your subscription and meet accounting and legal duties.
  • Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to deal with your request.

Deleting your data

Because there is no account, most of your data is on your phone: uninstall the app or clear its data to remove it. To ask us about data connected to your anonymous ID or purchases, email novakovskisolutions@gmail.com. We can only find data tied to an anonymous ID if you can tell us that ID, so server-side counters mainly expire on their own.

Data security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including keeping our AI service credentials on the server and never inside the app, verifying that requests come from the genuine app, and encrypting data in transit. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights under GDPR (European users)

Avo Cal is currently not offered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. If you are in the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. You can also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Where we rely on consent, for example for health-related information, you can withdraw it at any time. To exercise your rights, contact novakovskisolutions@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Your rights under CCPA (California residents)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to request its deletion, to opt out of its sale (we do not sell personal information), and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise them, contact novakovskisolutions@gmail.com. We verify your identity before processing a request and respond within 45 days.

Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact novakovskisolutions@gmail.com and we will take steps to remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. Changes are effective when posted.
