What Avo Cal does

Avo Cal helps you track the food you eat. You can add meals with a photo or a text description, chat with the Avo mascot, get a daily calorie target calculated from the details you enter, create AI meal plans, and follow your weight, streaks and milestones. There is no account: your food log and settings are stored on your phone.

Calorie and nutrient figures come from AI and can be wrong, especially for mixed dishes, hidden ingredients and unusual portion sizes.
